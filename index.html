Real User Story
👨‍🌾
Ramu, 45 years old
Farmer · Khunti, Jharkhand · Class 8 pass · Feature phone

A Farmer's Fever Changed How We Think About Healthcare

"I had fever for three days. The doctor is 40 km away. I thought it will go away. It didn't."
1
Ramu opens MediLink
Selects Hindi, taps the mic icon, says "बुखार है, सिर दर्द है" (I have fever and headache).
2
Gemini AI classifies urgency
Returns: MODERATE RISK — possible dengue, visit PHC within 24 hours. In plain Hindi.
3
Finds nearest PHC
MediLink shows Ranchi PHC — 6km away, free, open today. One-tap directions.
4
Scans prescribed medicine
Pharmacist gives him "Paracetamol". AsliDawa scan: ✓ AUTHENTIC. Ramu takes it safely.
5
Medicine reminder set
MediLink alerts him daily. He completes the course. Recovers fully. Earns +50 Health Points.
